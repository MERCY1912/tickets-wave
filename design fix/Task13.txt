You are a senior SaaS product designer working in 2026.

Your task is to design a modern ticket management system interface.

Design principles:
- clean
- minimal
- premium SaaS look
- calm colors
- strong spacing system
- modern typography
- subtle gradients
- micro-interactions ready
- AI-first product feeling

Target audience:
startup teams, support managers, tech companies.

Style references:
Linear, Notion, Vercel, Raycast, Intercom.

Avoid:
- heavy borders
- cluttered UI
- corporate enterprise look
- old Jira-style layouts

Design a smart visual highlighting system for tickets.

Highlights should indicate:
- high priority
- SLA risk
- negative sentiment

Design rules:
- no aggressive colors
- soft glow or accent line
- animated pulse for critical tickets
- minimal distraction

The system should guide attention, not overwhelm.

Inspiration:
Linear issue highlights
modern productivity tools
